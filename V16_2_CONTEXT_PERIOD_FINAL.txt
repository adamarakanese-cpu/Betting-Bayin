BETTING BAYIN PRE-BET — V16.2 FINAL POLISH

1) PERIOD-AWARE MARKETS
- Every visible market now carries a time scope: Regular Time / 1st Half / 2nd Half.
- Same market line in different periods is never merged or overwritten.
- Customer output examples:
  Regular Time — Over (1.5)
  1st Half — Over (0.5)
  2nd Half — Double Chance — 1X
- Visible half markets receive a conservative period Poisson model anchored to the actual screenshot price.
- Automatic result settlement never uses a full-time score to settle a half market.

2) ODDS DISPLAY POLICY
- Exact screenshot odds remain visible when the selected tip uses a real quoted price.
- Model-estimated/synthetic odds are never shown to customers.
- Hidden-market tips show the selected market and win chance only.
- Parlay output also hides estimated leg/combined prices.

3) DYNAMIC LEAGUE + TEAM CONTEXT
- Research now requests verified league scoring profile, team scoring/conceding profile,
  goal-line rates, BTTS rate, sample size, style tags and source URLs when available.
- Context Profile Engine combines verified aggregate data with verified recent results.
- High/low scoring environment can move expected goals, but the adjustment is bounded
  so small samples cannot dominate the independent strength/Elo model.
- Missing lower-league data is never invented. The engine falls back to verified recent
  results and generic football priors with lower confidence.
- Research cache preserves gathered match context for reuse.

4) CUSTOMER BRANDING
- Customer-facing title is always: 👑 BETTING BAYIN PRE-BET
- Internal version remains V16.2 for health/deployment diagnostics only.
