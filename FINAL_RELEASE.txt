BETTING BAYIN — V15.0 FINAL PRE-BET

FINAL RELEASE SCOPE
- Football PRE-BET only. Live screenshots are rejected.
- Screenshot-visible market/selection odds are the source of truth and always override model estimates.
- Double Chance customer labels are normalized to 1X / 12 / 2X.
- Hidden model-supported markets may be considered even when not visible in the screenshot.
- Hidden prices are NEVER presented as live 1XBET prices; they are labeled Estimated Odds Range.
- Internal ranking is probability/risk first. Long-shot EV cannot overpower safer supported markets.
- Sparse/friendly/youth competitions receive uncertainty shrinkage.
- Unsupported market families (for example cards/corners without statistical support) do not displace model-supported picks merely because the bookmaker price looks attractive.
- Model-supported market discovery includes 1X2, Double Chance, Totals 0.5/1.5/2.5/3.5/4.5, BTTS, team scoring/clean sheet, at-least-one-team-to-score, and conservative Correct Score candidates.
- Correct Score carries a strong risk penalty.

SINGLE -> PARLAY MEMORY
- Every final single tip is stored per Telegram user.
- Natural-language requests such as "ခုနက ၅ ပွဲကို မောင်းတွဲ" or "last 5 parlay" use that user's recent tip history.
- The N recent tips are a candidate pool; the engine selects the strongest 2-4 legs rather than forcing every match into the accumulator.
- Duplicate matches are removed.
- Low-probability legs are filtered when safer legs exist.
- Combined probability compounds leg probabilities; uncertainty compounds too.
- Actual odds stay actual. Any accumulator containing model-only prices is labeled Combined Estimated Odds.

PERSISTENCE
- Production should set DATABASE_URL to a persistent PostgreSQL database.
- SQLite remains a local/dev fallback only. Render free local disk is not reliable persistent storage across rebuilds/restarts.
- The same database stores subscription users, usage logs, and tip_history.

CUSTOMER OUTPUT
- Concise: match, league, bet type, final tip, odds/estimated range, win chance.
- No internal EV/edge/audit/model-debug text is shown to customers.

NOT CLAIMED
- No guaranteed win rate or "sure win" claim.
- No live 1XBET hidden-market price is invented.
- Cards/corners/other unsupported model families are not fabricated from no data.
