BETTING BAYIN — PRE-BET FINAL RELEASE V14.0

Release scope
- PRE-BET football only. Live screenshots are rejected before prediction.
- Customer output stays concise: match, competition, pre-bet, one tip, odds/estimated range, win chance.

Locked market/odds rules
1. Screenshot-visible market + selection => screenshot odds are the absolute source of truth.
2. Hidden/model-only market => never shown as a real bookmaker quote.
3. Hidden/model-only odds => shown as an Estimated Odds Range derived from model probability.
4. Double Chance customer labels => 1X / 12 / 2X only.
5. Alias matching prevents X2/2X, X1/1X, BTTS/Both Teams To Score, and common 1X2 naming from creating duplicate synthetic candidates.
6. Visible market candidates receive a modest source-quality preference because their prices are real screenshot quotes.

Prediction reliability controls
- Sparse evidence is blended toward market consensus for visible markets.
- Model-only probabilities are shrunk toward 50% when evidence is weak.
- Friendly/youth/reserve/regional/amateur/development competitions receive additional uncertainty penalties.
- Long-shot odds cannot dominate selection merely because nominal EV is large.
- Estimated-odds candidates receive no fake EV advantage.

Release checks
- Python compile: PASS

- Offline core self-test: PASS
- Secret-pattern scan: PASS at packaging time

Important
- Estimated odds are not live bookmaker prices.
- Football predictions are probabilistic; no win rate can be guaranteed.
- For persistent subscriptions on Render, configure DATABASE_URL (Postgres). Local SQLite on ephemeral hosting is not persistent.
