Production stack
- Telegram Pre-Bet screenshot input
- Vision extraction with visible-odds priority
- Research + probability + calibration + market ranking
- Hidden supported-market discovery with estimated odds ranges
- Single Tip output
- Persistent Single -> Parlay history
- Best 2-4 leg accumulator selection from recent tips
- PostgreSQL persistent subscription and history support via DATABASE_URL
- Telegram customer menu and admin support flow
- Verified result tracking
- Automatic performance measurement and bounded calibration feedback

Final production rules
1. Screenshot-visible odds always override estimated/synthetic odds for the same market selection.
2. Double Chance customer labels are 1X / 12 / 2X.
3. Hidden market prices are shown only as Estimated Odds Range.
4. Live screenshots are rejected by the Pre-Bet bot.
5. Low-data competitions receive uncertainty penalties.
6. Parlay never forces all supplied matches into the accumulator.
7. Result tracking never guesses an unverified final score.
8. Performance learning activates only after sample thresholds and is capped at +/-4pp.
9. Estimated odds are excluded from ROI accounting.
10. Customer tip delivery continues even if performance tracking is temporarily unavailable.
